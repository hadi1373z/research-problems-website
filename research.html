Does efficient testing imply efficient distance estimation?

Model

Fix a graph property P in the dense model. qP(ε) counts sampled vertices; dP(G) is normalized edit distance. An estimator distinguishes dP(G) ≤ α from dP(G) ≥ α+ε, for every threshold α, with probability at least 2/3.

Full question

If P is testable using qP(ε) sampled vertices, is it estimable using qP(ε′) vertices for some ε′ = poly(ε)? The polynomial rescaling may depend on P, but not on n or ε.

Literature snapshot

The 2025 survey's Theorem 5.3 gives a general doubly exponential conversion. Problem 5.4 asks for the much smaller rescaled tester bound.

Literature checked: 2026-10-31. Explicit open problem in the survey; subsequent-work searches found no resolution. This is a literature review, not an automatic status guarantee. A research snapshot from 31 October 2026 is now recorded below; no unrestricted resolution is claimed.

Remaining gap

The polynomial rescaling conversion for arbitrary graph properties is unproved. The edgeless calculation establishes a necessary loss, not a counterexample to the stated conjecture.

A quadratic sample loss is necessary in a simple property

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

For the edgeless property, optimal canonical testing uses Θ(1/ε) vertices and arbitrary-threshold distance estimation needs Θ(1/ε²).

Assumptions
  • Dense simple graphs; unordered edits divided by n².
  • Sample complexity counts vertices, and n may be arbitrarily large.
Argument

Independent pair samples give both upper bounds. Universal-core versus independent-set examples give the testing lower bound; relative entropy for two nearby core proportions gives the estimation lower bound.

Limitation

This property satisfies the conjectured conversion under ε ↦ ε². It does not refute every polynomial rescaling.

Is the optimal sample complexity of k-colorability Θ(log(k)/ε)?

Model

For k ≥ 2 and ε sufficiently small for the given k, let Sk(ε) be the least sample size such that a uniformly sampled induced subgraph of every graph ε-far from k-colorable is non-k-colorable with probability at least 2/3. Distance counts edge deletions divided by n².

Full question

Prove or disprove Sk(ε) = Θ(log(k)/ε), as conjectured by Kushnir and Shapira. Determine the sharp dependence on k and ε for the canonical one-sided tester.

Literature snapshot

Published September 2026 bounds are Ω(log(k)/ε) and O((k/ε) log(1/ε)). Xu v2, revised 26 October 2026, reports O(k log(k)/ε). This newer claim is a preprint, and still leaves a factor-k gap in the joint dependence.

Sharp sample size for complete (k+1)-partite graphs

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

For 0 < ε ≤ 1/(16k²), the worst-case sample size within this class is Θ(1/(kε) + log(k)/√ε).

Assumptions
  • Exactly k+1 positive independent parts, complete across parts, no extra isolates.
  • Uniform constants for k ≥ 2; sufficiently large blowups handle rounding.
Argument

Distance is the product of the two smallest part masses. Missing-part probabilities give the upper bound; one rare part and k equal rare parts give matching lower bounds.

Limitation

Is graph isomorphism fixed-parameter tractable in maximum degree?

Model

Both input graphs have n vertices and maximum degree at most d, with d≥2 (degrees 0 and 1 are elementary). Fixed-parameter tractability means time f(d) · n^C for a computable f and an absolute constant C independent of d.

Full question

Is there an isomorphism algorithm with running time f(d) · n^C for all n and d?

Literature snapshot

Grohe, Neuen and Schweitzer obtain n^{O((log d)^c)} time for an absolute c. The exponent still depends on d; Neuen's 2026 survey, §4.1 after Corollary 4.7, explicitly records FPT in maximum degree as open.

Literature checked: 2026-10-31. Explicit open-status statement in the survey; no later FPT resolution was located. A research snapshot from 31 October 2026 is now recorded below; no unrestricted resolution is claimed.

Remaining gap

An FPT algorithm in the number of branching vertices does not imply FPT in maximum degree: a cubic graph may have every vertex branching.

Can equal-density cospectral graphons resist finite cospectral approximation?

Model

U,W are graphons with t(Ck,U)=t(Ck,W) for every k≥3 and ∫U=∫W. Here t is homomorphism density, Ck is a cycle, and δ□ is cut distance after measure-preserving relabeling. WG is the step graphon representing G.

Full question

Do such U,W exist for which no finite simple cospectral graph pairs Gj,Hj of equal order tending to infinity satisfy δ□(WGj,U)→0 and δ□(WHj,W)→0? Cospectral finite graphs have identical adjacency spectra, including multiplicities.

Literature snapshot

The paper proves inapproximability when ∫U≠∫W. Equal edge density rules out that particular obstruction and is the remaining published question.

Literature checked: 2026-10-31. Explicitly open in the published paper; targeted later-work searches found no resolution. The preprint numbers it Problem 4.4. A research snapshot from 31 October 2026 is now recorded below; no unrestricted resolution is claimed.

Remaining gap

A resistant equal-density pair is not known here. Approximation of limiting moments is insufficient: every finite pair must have exactly the same characteristic polynomial.

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

Assumptions

Narrow the quantitative gap in triangle removal

Model

Let T(G) count unordered triangles and τ△(G) be the fewest edge deletions making G triangle-free. Define δ△(ε)=inf T(G)/n³ over all positive integers n and all eligible n-vertex graphs with τ△(G)≥εn². tower(h) denotes a tower of twos of height h.

Full question

Determine sharper asymptotic upper or lower bounds for δ△(ε) as ε→0, reducing the gap between the known construction and removal guarantees.

Literature snapshot

Fox's removal argument gives a tower of height O(log(1/ε)) for 1/δ△. Behrend-type constructions give exp(Ω(log²(1/ε))); Hunter's 2025 construction improves the constant while retaining this form.

Exact complete-tripartite behavior

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

For a complete tripartite core plus isolates, τ/n² = min(ab,ac,bc) and T/n³ = abc; ε-farness implies T ≥ ε^(3/2)n³.

Assumptions

Argument

Limitation

This does not improve general triangle removal; the exponent is sharp only within the stated class.

Does induced C₄ removal admit a polynomial bound?

Model

An induced C₄ is a set of four vertices spanning a cycle with no diagonal edges. Distance to induced-C₄-freeness allows both edge additions and deletions, normalized by n².

Full question

Do absolute constants a,b>0 exist such that every n-vertex graph requiring at least εn² edge edits to become induced-C₄-free contains at least aε^b n⁴ induced copies of C₄?

Literature snapshot

Gishboliner and Shapira prove a lower bound 2^{−(1/ε)^C} n⁴ for some absolute C>0. Gishboliner's 2025 notes restate the polynomial improvement as Conjecture 3.6. Labeled versus unlabeled copies changes only the constant factor.

Literature checked: 2026-10-31. Explicit conjecture in the author's notes; 2025–2026 searches found no polynomial removal theorem settling it. A research snapshot from 31 October 2026 is now recorded below; no unrestricted resolution is claimed.

Remaining gap

The polynomial guarantee was proved only for triangle-free inputs. In general, ordinary four-cycles need not be induced, and edge deletion can create induced copies.

Every triangle-free n-vertex graph ε-far under arbitrary edits from induced-C4-freeness has at least (ε^5/4)n^4 induced copies.

A direct root-n sampling bound for local networks

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

For a bounded ordered-k local kernel |h| ≤ M, E|N_n−N_m| ≤ Mk(n^(−1/2)+m^(−1/2)) + Mk(k−1)(n^(−1)+m^(−1)).

Assumptions
  • Fixed k and n,m ≥ k; normalized averages over all ordered tuples.
  • Independent simple loopless graph-signals sampled from one fixed graphon-signal.
  • The kernel depends only on the tuple’s internal edges and signals.
Argument

Injective tuple kernels on disjoint vertex sets are independent, so their average has variance at most M²k²/n. Noninjective tuples change the average by at most Mk(k−1)/n.

Limitation

The limit integrates Bernoulli edge randomness; it need not equal the same formula evaluated directly on W. The result does not cover a nonlinear activation after global pooling.

An explicit coefficient-dependent bound at fixed polynomial depth

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

A finite motif expansion has transfer bound A(n^(−1/2)+m^(−1/2)) + B(n^(−1)+m^(−1)), with A = Σ|c_j|r^D_j v_j and B = Σ|c_j|r^D_j v_j(v_j−1).

Assumptions
  • A finite loopless multigraph motif expansion is supplied.
  • Signals are bounded by r; model parameters are independent of sample size.
Argument

Apply the local-kernel bound to each motif. Collapse parallel edges in the Bernoulli sampling limit.

Limitation

This may have very large expansion constants. It is not a tight depth/width/order theorem.

No common rate shape for all continuous activations

needs-independent-review · novelty: unassessed

For any proposed positive rate tending to zero, some single fixed continuous activation and fixed network violate an O(r_n+r_m) transfer bound.

Assumptions

Argument
